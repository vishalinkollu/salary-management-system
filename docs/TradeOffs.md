# Trade-Offs

## Why SQLite?

Pros
- Lightweight
- Easy setup
- Perfect for assessment

Cons
- Not ideal for large production workloads

## Why Prisma?

Pros
- Type-safe queries
- Easy migrations
- Cleaner code

## Why Pagination?

10,000 employees cannot be loaded at once.

Pagination improves:

- Performance
- UX
- API response times

## Why Not Authentication?

Assessment focused on salary management workflows.

Authentication was intentionally left out to maximize delivery of core business functionality.
